Taipei

Capital city blending tradition with modern life.

Taipei (台北, Táiběi) is the capital and largest city of Taiwan. A modern metropolis with a vibrant culture, Taipei seamlessly blends traditional Chinese heritage with contemporary urban life. The city is known for its bustling night markets, towering skyscrapers, and rich culinary scene. From the iconic Taipei 101 to ancient temples and hot springs, Taipei offers an incredible mix of attractions. The city serves as the political, economic, and cultural center of Taiwan, making it an essential destination for any visitor to the island.
